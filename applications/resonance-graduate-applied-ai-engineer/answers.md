## Short note: something I built with AI
At the Max Planck Institute I wanted to know whether an LLM can notice when a closed-loop conversation has become repetitive, and recover. I generated 10,000 self-conversations, labelled them with a 31B LLM judge (95% of labels validated) to get 8,504 examples, then fully fine-tuned Qwen2.5-3B over five versions on an HPC cluster using SLURM and vLLM. The model detects collapse in unseen conversations, was preferred over the previous best in 11 of 15 paired runs, and cut empty turns from 2.8% to 0.4%. Long 500-turn runs showed the recovery is only temporary, which I reported as it was.
